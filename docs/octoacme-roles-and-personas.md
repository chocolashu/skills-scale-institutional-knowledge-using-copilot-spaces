# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical vision and guidance to ensure the solution aligns with system architecture, scalability requirements, and technical standards. They collaborate with product and development teams to evaluate feasibility and drive technical excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Review solution designs for architectural alignment and scalability
- Evaluate technical feasibility during planning and estimation
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers on coding standards and best practices
- Coordinate with DevOps and infrastructure teams on deployment requirements

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically sound, maintainable, and scalable
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Foster a culture of technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- PR reviews and code feedback
- Risk and dependency escalations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers** — Reviews designs, provides guidance on implementation approach
- **With Product Managers** — Advises on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- **With Project Managers** — Identifies technical risks and dependencies for the risk register
- **With QA Lead** — Defines testability requirements and quality gates

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They work closely with the development team to establish quality standards, define test approaches, and ensure acceptance criteria are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Create and maintain test plans for features and releases
- Identify testability requirements and quality gaps early
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Track defects and ensure closure before release
- Communicate quality status and risks to project and product teams

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Build quality into the development process early

### Typical Communication
- Test planning meetings during sprint planning
- QA status updates in standups and weekly syncs
- Defect reports and test result summaries
- Quality gate reviews before release

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers** — Defines testability requirements, collaborates on test approach
- **With Technical Lead** — Aligns on quality gates and design for testability
- **With Product Managers** — Validates acceptance criteria and user story requirements
- **With Project Managers** — Reports quality risks and release readiness
- **With Release Manager** — Coordinates pre-release testing and sign-off

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key Stakeholders provide business context, secure resources, and ensure executive alignment. They remove organizational blockers and serve as champions for project success.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve scope, timeline, and budget
- Remove organizational and cross-team blockers
- Provide executive visibility and escalation path
- Review project status and make go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI
- Ensure executive alignment and support
- Reduce organizational friction and blockers

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone review and approval meetings
- Escalation for budget and resource decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers** — Reviews status, approves changes, escalates blockers
- **With Product Managers** — Aligns on business objectives and priorities
- **With Developers** — Provides business context during kickoff and demos

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release timelines, and ensure compliance with deployment checklists. They serve as the central point of coordination for moving features from development to production.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment activities and release scheduling
- Ensure release checklists and pre-deployment requirements are met
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams
- Coordinate rollback and incident response if needed
- Maintain release notes and deployment documentation
- Track and coordinate dependencies across teams for releases

### Goals
- Ensure smooth, predictable releases with minimal risk
- Reduce deployment-related incidents and rework
- Maintain clear communication on release status and timelines

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Pre-release checklist verification
- Release announcements and post-deployment verifications
- Incident communication and rollback coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers** — Coordinates release timing and milestone alignment
- **With QA Lead** — Ensures pre-release testing is complete and quality gates are met
- **With Technical Lead** — Coordinates technical deployment requirements and rollback plans
- **With Developers** — Communicates deployment procedures and post-deployment verification steps
